- The documentation pages state their required areas (ADR-0017 section 2.1) and the gate measures them: `caos-shell-gate` fails (`ADR-0017.2`) an Introduction, Methodology, Implementation,
  Experiments or Benchmark page that lacks a named area or whose area lacks the content it requires, in either language. The Introduction opens with an executive view and a technical view.
  The areas, their rules and the floor ids are in `docs/04_views.md`, from one table (`gate/areas.mjs`).
- `DocSection` takes `area` (and `kind` for a method), `DocPage` takes `floorWaivers` (a waiver needs a reason of 12 letters or digits in two words or more and is listed in the gate report), `Callout` renders `data-callout`,
  and the new `Glossary`, `Stat` and `Question` components render `data-glossary`, `data-stat` and `data-qa`.
- The content checks of the documentation pages count only what a reader can see: rendered, opaque, inside the page, not clipped, on the rendered text (a paragraph, statement, glossary item or answer hidden by transparency, off-screen positioning or a clipping box is not counted).
- The gate self-test grows from 41 to 95 plants, one per floor and per trap (the areas, the reader-visibility rules and the rules found by the gate review of 0.11.0), and three legitimate pages that must pass; the fixture product carries every area.
- The documentation checks follow ADR-0017 section 2.1 as amended on 2026-10-12: the executive view must be the view the Introduction opens on; a section outside the two views, or an equation or a glossary shown anywhere while the executive view is open, fails; a glossary item, a step and a quantified statement count only when a reader sees them (the unit and the source of a `Stat` must be readable); the rows of `outcomes` may be a list; a method must cite references; the `live` area needs a control registered with `data-control`; a waiver reason is judged on 12 letters or digits in two words; G13 runs in every language on the documentation routes; a tab list inside a `DocSection` belongs to the section (its panels are not topics, and the section is judged across every state the walk visited).
- The language of a page stays a reviewer's duty (`docs/07_gate.md`).
