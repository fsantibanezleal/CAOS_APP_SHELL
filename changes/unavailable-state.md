- The gate accepts a declared, explained unavailable view: an instrument that draws nothing and holds a `data-state="unavailable"` element a reader can see (rendered, opaque, a box of 40 by 12 px at least inside the instrument and inside every box that clips it) with a reason in its rendered text (12 letters or digits in two words at least) passes G6 and still gets every other check. A transparent, zero-size, off-screen, screen-reader-only, zero-font or clipped note fails G6 ("no reader can see the note"); a reason of punctuation, whitespace or hidden text fails G6 naming the missing reason; a blank chart beside the note, or any drawing element rendered in the instrument (collapsed or invisible too), fails G6 ("a drawing is present but not visible"); a `display: none` note does not exempt. The gate checks that a reason exists, not that it is true (refs #99).
