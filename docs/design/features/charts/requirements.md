# Requirements: charts

Status: live

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the test or the gate plant that fails when it is violated. A behaviour with no test is not a row here: it is listed as a gap in the delegation report and gets its row when its test exists.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| SHC-001 | THE UPlotChart host SHALL declare its series count (`data-series`), its axis titles (`data-axis-titles`) and its drawing state (`data-drawn`), and SHALL show a hint at rest. | `test/base07.test.tsx::S12: the chart host declares its series, axis titles and drawing state` |
| SHC-002 | WHERE a chart has two or more series, THE UPlotChart SHALL key every series under the plot, with the dash and the mode of each, and WHERE it has one series THE UPlotChart SHALL show no key. | `test/base07.test.tsx::known defect 17: a chart of two or more series keys every series, always; one series needs no key` |
| SHC-003 | WHERE an axis has fixed decimals, THE UPlotChart SHALL tick only where its labels differ, `tickSteps` SHALL give integer steps for integer axes, and the host SHALL declare the number of repeated labels in `data-ticks-repeat`. | `test/base07.test.tsx::known defect 21: an axis with fixed decimals ticks only where its labels differ, and the host declares repeats` |
| SHC-004 | WHEN a tick label repeats the label before it, THE gate SHALL fail under G6. | `test/gate-selftest.mjs::repeat-ticks` |
| SHC-005 | THE `sortOrder` function SHALL return null for a sorted or empty x and the permutation for an unsorted x, keeping ties in order, so that every index maps back to the caller's. | `test/base09.test.tsx::sortOrder: uPlot draws by increasing x; an unsorted x is sorted and every index maps back` |
| SHC-006 | THE `parityRange` function SHALL give one padded range over both axes, ignore absent values and still give a range for a single value. | `test/base09.test.tsx::parityRange: one padded range over both axes, absent values ignored` |
| SHC-007 | WHERE a chart has more than six series, THE `seriesStyle` function SHALL draw a repeated colour dashed, and an explicit colour SHALL stay the caller's. | `test/base09.test.tsx::seriesStyle: past the six colours of the rotation, a repeated colour is dashed` |
| SHC-008 | THE UPlotChart host SHALL declare a parity plot (`data-parity`), a log x axis (`data-log-x`) and picking (`data-pick`). | `test/base09.test.tsx::UPlotChart declares a parity plot, a log x axis and picking for the gate and the stylesheet` |
| SHC-009 | THE `nearestPoint` function SHALL give the point nearest the pointer in the plane over every series, and no point where a position is null. | `test/base10.test.tsx::nearestPoint: the point nearest in the plane, over every series; null positions are no point` |
| SHC-010 | WHEN the pointer is over a parity plot, THE UPlotChart SHALL name and pick the point nearest in the plane, and the readout SHALL name only the series that has that point. | `e2e/parity.test.ts::a parity plot names and picks the point nearest the pointer in the plane, and draws its reference line` |
| SHC-011 | THE parity plot SHALL tick both axes at one step, be a square box, and WHEN its box is taller than wide centre the square down as well as across. | `e2e/parity.test.ts::a parity plot ticks both axes at one step, and fills a tall box centred in it` |
| SHC-012 | THE UPlotChart host SHALL declare its reference lines (`data-y-marks`). | `test/base10.test.tsx::UPlotChart declares its reference lines` |
| SHC-013 | THE `rangeWithLevels` function SHALL widen the data's extent to a level above or below it and leave it alone when the levels lie inside. | `test/ymarks.test.ts::rangeWithLevels: a level above the data raises the top, a level below lowers the bottom`, `test/ymarks.test.ts::rangeWithLevels: levels inside the data leave the extent as it is` |
| SHC-014 | WHERE a chart has no data, THE `rangeWithLevels` function SHALL take the levels alone, and with no data and no level SHALL give no range. | `test/ymarks.test.ts::rangeWithLevels: a chart with no data takes the levels alone; no data and no level has no range` |
| SHC-015 | WHERE the axis is logarithmic, THE `rangeWithLevels` function SHALL ignore a level at or below 0 and take in the others. | `test/ymarks.test.ts::rangeWithLevels: on a log axis a level at 0 or below is ignored, the others are taken in` |
| SHC-016 | WHEN a level pushes an end out, THE `rangeWithLevels` function SHALL move that end a further share of the span, and the other end SHALL stay. | `test/ymarks.test.ts::rangeWithLevels: an end a level pushed out moves a further share of the span; the other end stays` |
| SHC-017 | THE `rangeWithLevels` function SHALL ignore a level that is not finite. | `test/ymarks.test.ts::rangeWithLevels: a level that is not finite is ignored` |
| SHC-018 | THE reference line tones `warn`, `bad`, `good` and `neutral` SHALL each map to their shell colour token (`YMARK_TONE_TOKENS`). | `test/ymarks.test.ts::every tone of a reference line has its shell colour token` |
| SHC-019 | WHEN a reference line has a tone, THE UPlotChart SHALL draw the line and its label in the tone's colour, a line without a tone SHALL keep the warn colour, and a bad line above the data and a neutral line below it SHALL be drawn near the top and the bottom of the plot. | `e2e/ymarks.test.ts::a bad line above the data and a neutral line below it are drawn in their tone (${theme})` |
| SHC-020 | THE parity plot SHALL draw its reference line at the level given (a null model's constant). | `e2e/parity.test.ts::a parity plot names and picks the point nearest the pointer in the plane, and draws its reference line` |
| SHC-021 | THE `drawMarks` function SHALL draw each label left-aligned, inside the plot, haloed, on the side of its line where it fits, on the next row when it would overlap, and SHALL NOT draw a mark outside the x range. | `test/base08.test.tsx::BL-013: a mark label is left-aligned, inside the plot, haloed, and never on another label` |
| SHC-022 | THE `logDecades` function SHALL give powers of ten, at most eight, every one labelled, below the tick table floor `LOG_TABLE_FLOOR` of 1e-22, and none for a non-positive or inverted range. | `test/base08.test.tsx::defect 31: a log axis below the tick table of uPlot ticks at powers of ten, at most eight, every one labelled` |
| SHC-023 | WHEN a log axis reaches below the tick table of uPlot, THE UPlotChart SHALL draw without an error, on the y axis and on the x axis. | `e2e/charts.test.ts::a log axis down to ${lo} draws without an error (known shell defect 31)`, `e2e/charts.test.ts::a log x axis down to ${lo} draws without an error (known shell defect 31)` |
| SHC-024 | THE text kit SHALL fit a label in at most two lines broken at a word and shortened last, round ticks so that they cover the range with a count the labels leave room for, and give `niceStep` as 1, 2, 2.5 or 5 times a power of ten. | `test/base08.test.tsx::BL-014 and BL-015: the text kit fits labels and rounds ticks; the bar chart declares itself for the gate` |
| SHC-025 | THE BarChart SHALL declare itself for the gate (`data-chart`) and draw nothing before its box has a width (`data-drawn=0`). | `test/base08.test.tsx::BL-014 and BL-015: the text kit fits labels and rounds ticks; the bar chart declares itself for the gate` |
| SHC-026 | WHEN nothing is drawn, a stage is under 30 percent painted, or a drawing under the floor sits under its note, THE gate SHALL fail under G6. | `test/gate-selftest.mjs::blank`, `test/gate-selftest.mjs::small`, `test/gate-selftest.mjs::stage-floor` |
| SHC-027 | WHEN a label of a chart or figure is cut at the drawing's edge, lies outside it, overlaps another, or is cut only in the wide fallback font, THE gate SHALL fail under G10. | `test/gate-selftest.mjs::label-cut`, `test/gate-selftest.mjs::label-overlap`, `test/gate-selftest.mjs::label-wide` |
| SHC-028 | THE G6 arithmetic SHALL find a drawn rectangle exactly, treat a uniform region as blank and not double count unions. | `test/gate.test.ts::G6 arithmetic: a drawn rectangle is found exactly, a uniform region is blank, unions do not double count` |
| SHC-029 | WHILE the viewport is 1280 px wide or wider, THE filling PlotCard SHALL keep a drawing of at least the stage floor (140 px) when two or three filling cards share a short column, in the wide fallback font and with a note at its cap; below 1280 px its layout SHALL not change. | `e2e/fill-floor.test.ts::stacked filling cards keep the stage floor at ${size} in ${lang} with the wide font` |
| SHC-030 | WHERE the x axis is logarithmic, THE `decadeOnly` function SHALL keep the powers of ten as the ticks that get a label, give null for every other tick and keep null as null. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::decadeOnly: a log axis keeps the labels of the powers of ten and blanks the rest; null stays null` |
| SHC-031 | WHEN a log axis holds no power of ten, THE `decadeOnly` function SHALL keep its ticks, so the axis is never left unlabelled. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::decadeOnly: a range with no decade in it keeps its ticks, so the axis is never left unlabelled` |
| SHC-032 | THE decade labels of a log axis SHALL follow the interface language (1,000 in English, 1.000 in Spanish) and every other tick SHALL be blank. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::decade labels follow the interface language: 1,000 in English, 1.000 in Spanish, a blank for every other tick` |
| SHC-033 | WHERE the x axis is logarithmic, THE UPlotChart SHALL label every power of ten inside the range and no other tick, and the host SHALL declare the labels shown (`data-log-labels`). | `e2e/log-parity.test.ts::a log x axis labels its decades and nothing else, no decade in range missing` |
| SHC-034 | THE `parityBox` function SHALL give the side of the square box as the smaller of the box width and the height asked for, or of the box height when the chart fills it. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::parityBox: the side is the smaller of the box width and the height asked for, or of the box height when it fills` |
| SHC-035 | THE parity plot SHALL be a square box with the same ticks on both axes. | `e2e/log-parity.test.ts::a parity plot is a square box with the same ticks on both axes` |
| SHC-036 | WHEN two marks lie close on a log axis, THE `drawMarks` function SHALL print their labels apart. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::drawMarks: a 60 cm target beside a 100 cm limit on a log axis print their labels apart` |
| SHC-037 | THE `clampMark` function SHALL keep a dragged value inside the range of its line, snap it to the step and give the lower end for a value that is not a number. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::clampMark: a value stays inside the range and snaps to the step` |
| SHC-038 | WHEN a key is pressed on a draggable line, THE `nudgeMark` function SHALL move it one step for an arrow, ten for a Page key, to an end for Home and End, stop at the ends of its range and give null for any other key. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::nudgeMark: the arrows move one step, Page keys ten, Home and End go to the ends; other keys do nothing` |
| SHC-039 | WHERE the y axis of a draggable line is logarithmic, THE `nudgeMark` function SHALL move it by a factor of a hundredth of the range in decades. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::nudgeMark: on a log axis a step is a factor, a hundredth of the range in decades` |
| SHC-040 | WHEN the reader drags the handle of a draggable reference line (`yMarks[i].drag`), THE UPlotChart SHALL report each new y through `onYMarkChange`, keep it inside the range and expose the handle as a vertical slider with its value. | `e2e/log-parity.test.ts::a draggable horizontal marker follows the pointer within its range and reports each value` |
| SHC-041 | WHEN the handle of a draggable reference line has focus, THE UPlotChart SHALL move the line with the arrows, the Page keys, Home and End and keep the handle on the line. | `e2e/log-parity.test.ts::a draggable horizontal marker moves with the keyboard: arrows, Page keys, Home and End` |
| SHC-042 | THE `overlayExtent` function SHALL give the x and y extent of the series that bring an x of their own, ignoring absent and non-finite values, and none when there is no such point. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::overlayExtent: the x and y of the series that bring their own x, null and non-finite values ignored` |
| SHC-043 | WHERE a points series has an x of its own, THE UPlotChart SHALL draw every point inside the plot on that x, the axes taking its extent in, and the host SHALL declare the points drawn (`data-overlay-drawn`). | `e2e/log-parity.test.ts::measured points on their own x are drawn inside the plot, the axis taking in the ones past the curve` |
| SHC-044 | THE UPlotChart host SHALL declare its draggable lines (`data-y-drag`) and its overlays (`data-overlay`), and only a draggable line SHALL have a handle. | `test/uplotLogParity.test.tsx::UPlotChart declares its draggable lines and its overlay for the gate; a chart without them declares neither`, `e2e/log-parity.test.ts::only a draggable line has a handle, and the host declares how many` |
